One-click leveraged borrow, inside the pool’s limits

Borrow USDT, buy more of the same stock on PancakeSwap and post it as collateral.

  1. Borrow USDT

    An ordinary pool borrow, within the stock’s current limit.

  2. Swap to bStock

    0.5% slippage limit. No swap if the price is over 2% worse than the oracle.

  3. Supply it

    The new collateral makes room for the next round.

One click

One EIP-5792 batch, simulated first. All steps run or none do. Ten calls fit about two rounds.

Step by step

Up to five rounds. A stopped run offers to repay or resupply what is left in the wallet.

The same screen reduces or closes a position. Borrowing still needs USDT in the pool, healthy keeper reports and an eligible wallet.

Five steps from live prototype to open borrowing

  1. Calibrate risk

    Set discounts, limits and caps from historical gap data and the depth liquidators can actually sell into.

  2. Activate automation

    Run the keeper and eligibility service on the live pool, configure the app to use it and add a liquidation bot.

  3. Confirm price feeds

    Confirm with Atlas which feed instances to use, and decide on a second price source.

  4. Review and license

    Security review of the pool changes. Confirm Aave V3.6 license eligibility; it converts to MIT by March 6, 2027.

  5. Open small

    Move admin to a multisig, fund a deficit backstop, verify issuer transfer rules, then add USDT under low caps.
