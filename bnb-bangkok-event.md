# BNB Bangkok: Build, Demo & Pitch (Nov 25-27, 2026)

**Type:** Hackathon and demo night, plus a startup pitch track
**Compiled:** Nov 13, 2026 (updated Nov 19)
**Source:** https://www.bnbchain.org/en/blog/bnb-bangkok-build-demo-and-pitch (BNB Chain blog, published Nov 6, 2026). Both Luma pages were also read on Nov 13, 2026.

## Overview

BNB Chain is hosting a hackathon and demo night in Bangkok focused on **RWA, Trading and AI**, for builders, founders and investors.

* **Dates:** Nov 25-27, 2026
* **Venue:** Park Hyatt Bangkok, Central Embassy, 88 Thanon Witthayu, Pathum Wan, Bangkok 10330
* **Demo Night:** Fri Nov 27 (Bangkok time, UTC+7). The BNB Chain blog says 3:00-6:00 pm, but the Luma page says 2:00-8:00 pm (main stage demos and awards at 3:00 pm, closing party 5:30 pm, event ends 6:00 pm). Plan around 2:00-8:00 pm until the organizers clarify.
* **Build start:** the blog says the 36-hour build starts in the evening of Nov 25. A listing site shows the event opening at 12:00 on Nov 25.
* **Sponsors listed on Luma:** Flap.sh, Four.meme, AWS, Atlas Oracle

## Developer Track

* 36-hour build, Nov 25 to the morning of Nov 27. Teams present at Demo Night.
* Open to builders in AI, RWA or Trading.
* **Judging:** Design and Usability, Scalability, Innovation, Open Source, Integration.
* **Prizes:**
  * 1st: $5,000 plus up to $50,000 Kickstart support
  * 2nd: $3,000
  * 3rd: $2,000
* All top finishers get a fast-track to the **YZi Labs EASY Residency** and access to the "$1B Builder Fund".

## Startup Track

* For existing, mature projects: a live product, user traction and investment readiness.
* 3 projects are selected to pitch to VCs.
* Fast-track to the EASY Residency and potential investment through the Builder Fund.
* You can join both tracks. If you do, you must clearly separate new work from existing work in the presentation.

## How to apply

* **Developer Track:** https://luma.com/n5y1y7cu
* **Startup Track and Demo Night:** https://luma.com/5topfryz (the free attendee ticket is for watching, the Startup Track ticket is for pitching)
* Everyone applies individually, even if they have a team, and must use the same team name as their teammates.
* Bring a GitHub or portfolio link. Startup teams must show a live product with traction.
