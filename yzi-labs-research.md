# Ideas from YZi Labs

Research on what YZi Labs (ex-Binance Labs) and BNB Chain fund and want next. Compiled Nov 25, 2026 from primary sources (YZi Labs blog and EASY Residency page, fetched live) plus news cross-checks.

## Key takeaways

* Funding mix per CZ (Consensus Miami, May 2026): ~70% blockchain, 20% AI, 10% biotech.
* 2026 deals by category (DefiLlama, 50 total): DeFi 13, AI 5, Payments 5, Trading 4, Prediction Market 3, RWA 3, Banking 3. Generic lending, perps and prediction markets are a minority. Payments/FX, AI agents, RWA and trading infrastructure dominate.
* Recommendation: avoid generic lending, perps and prediction-market clones. Build against stated gaps (below).

## EASY Residency S5: stated priorities

Source: YZi Labs blog, Aug 14, 2026.

**Programmable capital and onchain markets**

* Stablecoin payments, FX and treasury infra (continued from S4). Includes agent wallets, delegated spending, usage-based payments.
* Exchange-grade onchain markets: high-performance order books, matching and liquidation engines, unified margin, trading appchains. App layer: proof-based collateral, privacy-preserving account attestations, cross-venue margin.
* DeFi layer for tokenized equities and RWAs (RWA-collateralized lending).
* Institutional onchain options, privacy, post-quantum migration.

**AI infrastructure**

* Compute marketplaces, compute capacity and hedging markets.
* Trusted agent infrastructure (permission and accountability for autonomous agents).
* Physical-AI data.

**AI interfaces**

* Privacy-first memory layers, voice-first agents.

**AI x Bio**

* Drug discovery, clinical AI, agentic lab automation, DeSci.

The EASY page adds this season's focus: "AI inside markets", meaning models and agents that price, route and execute in live venues, plus the execution, data and settlement infrastructure they depend on.
