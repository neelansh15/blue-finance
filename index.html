Dawn Finance

Borrow stablecoins against tokenized US stocks, with a risk layer that knows when the market is open.

Isolated lending markets on unmodified Morpho Blue, on BNB Chain. Tokens trade 24/7; the stock market does not. Dawn prices that gap in.

How Dawn works Read the architecture README

The problem

Tokens trade 24/7. The stock market does not.

A tokenized stock can change hands on a Saturday, but the US equity it tracks only trades in set sessions. When the market reopens, the price can jump past where it closed. Lending protocols built for always-on crypto assets treat every hour the same and ignore that gap risk.

  • OvernightThe market closes each evening. News lands before the next open.
  • WeekendsTwo days with no primary-market price discovery, then one reopen.
  • Holidays and haltsExchange holidays, trading halts and suspensions stretch the gap further.

How Dawn works

A risk layer on top of Morpho Blue

Dawn does not fork the lending core. It creates isolated markets on the canonical, unmodified Morpho Blue deployment and puts its logic in the oracle, a router and offchain keepers.

Isolated markets on Morpho Blue

One market per stock and loan asset. Bad debt in one market never touches another. Accounting, borrow, repay and liquidation stay in Morpho Blue, unmodified.

Market-hours-aware pricing

A regime registry tracks open, extended, closed, weekend or holiday, halted and corporate-action states. The oracle applies a haircut per regime, so borrowing power shrinks when the market is shut, and ramps down before the close instead of stepping.

Conservative oracle

Collateral is valued at the more conservative of the primary price feed and a DEX TWAP. If a source is stale or missing, an extra haircut applies. In the normal path the oracle never reverts, so liquidations keep working.

Stock-specific events

Splits via display multipliers, issuer pauses and whitelists, trading halts and reinvested dividends. Around these events new borrows pause and the price freezes to the last good value for a bounded time, so a split does not look like a crash.

Stablecoin-only borrowing

In v1 you post a tokenized stock and borrow stablecoins. That keeps risk one-sided: only the collateral price matters, with one oracle and one rate curve per market.

Liquidation

Morpho Blue liquidation stays permissionless. Dawn plans opt-in pre-liquidation for smaller, gentler partial liquidations, and runs its own backstop bot with no special privileges.

Roadmap

Start narrow, add leverage later

Draft plan. Nothing here is live or scheduled.

  1. v1 Prototype, BNB Bangkok

    Stablecoin borrowing

    Post a tokenized US stock, borrow stablecoins, with market-hours-aware limits and a conservative oracle. The hackathon demo runs on a testnet or mainnet fork.

  2. v1.5 Later

    Leverage vault

    Leveraged long positions on tokenized stocks, built on the same isolated markets and risk layer.

  3. v2 Later

    Shorting

    Borrowing the equity tokens themselves, which needs borrowable equity-token supply.
